行く and イク are the same sound. They are not the same word ⚠️

行く means to go. どこ行く is where are we going. カラオケ行く is shall we go to karaoke. You will use it every day.

イク is that same sound written in katakana, and in katakana it means to cum. Japanese uses katakana to show that a word is not being used in its normal sense.

So if you text someone 今イキます, you are not telling them that you are on your way.

Write it in kanji. 行きます. Your phone offers you both spellings and it does not warn you.
