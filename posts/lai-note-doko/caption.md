Where is ___ ? is the one sentence I would learn first ⚡

はどこですか means where is it. You put the thing you are looking for in front and you are done. 駅はどこですか is where is the station, ATMはどこですか is where is the ATM, and it keeps working the same way for 薬局, バス停 and 病院.

The part nobody teaches you is the answer. You ask it fine and then get この道をまっすぐ行ってください or 次の角を右に曲がってすぐです back at full speed, so I put the real replies on the pages too.

It is still beach weather here and I am still the one asking, three or four times a week.
