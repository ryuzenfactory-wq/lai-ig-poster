結構です. Kekkou desu. It sounds like a compliment, but it usually means no.

結構 on its own does mean fine or excellent, which is exactly why this one catches people out.

You can say it to turn down a bag at the konbini, and you will hear it when someone offers you more food.

You can also use it to turn down your change. お釣りは結構です. Otsuri wa kekkou desu. In a taxi, that means the driver keeps the change.

Save this before your next konbini visit ⚡
