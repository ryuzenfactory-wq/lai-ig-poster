ください or お願いします? Here is the rule ⚡

ください is for the thing. You name what you want handed to you and add it. 生ビールをください, レシートをください, スプーンください.

でお願いします is for the how. They gave you a choice and you are picking one. カウンター席でお願いします, PayPayでお願いします, 別々でお願いします.

That is the whole thing. One dinner uses both: you pick the seat with で, you order the beer with ください, you ask for no pork with で, you split the bill with で.

And if you put a verb in front instead of a thing, ください turns into do this for me. 温めてください. ここで止めてください.
