The izakaya says three things to you before you even open the menu ⚡

何名様ですか is how many people, and holding up your fingers is a perfectly normal answer. こちら、お通しです is the one that confuses everyone: a small dish you never ordered, that you will be charged for. It is not a scam and it is not a mistake, it is closer to a seating charge. And ご注文はお決まりですか just means are you ready to order.

Nobody warns you about the お通し, so people spend the whole meal wondering what that 400 yen was.
