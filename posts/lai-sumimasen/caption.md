すみません is not only sorry. It has three jobs, and most people only ever learn the first one.

Number one. Sorry. You step on someone's foot on the train, you say it.

Number two. Excuse me. You call a waiter with it. You pass in front of people with it.

Number three. Thank you. Someone picks up something you dropped, and you say it.

The third one is the one nobody explains. ありがとう thanks the person. すみません says that you noticed the trouble you made, and that is warmer than it looks.
