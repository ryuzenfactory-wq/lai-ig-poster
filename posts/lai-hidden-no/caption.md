Nobody tells you that a Japanese no does not sound like a no ⚡

ちょっと means a little, but when someone says it and then just stops, that is a refusal. むずかしいです means difficult, and difficult is how people here say no. かんがえておきます means I will think about it, and at work it almost always means the answer is already no.

None of these sound like rejection in English, so people keep pushing and the whole room gets awkward. Once you hear them properly, you save everyone the trouble.
