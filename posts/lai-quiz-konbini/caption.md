Four lines you will hear at a Japanese register today ⚡

温めますか, 袋はご利用ですか, ポイントカードはお持ちですか, 何かお探しですか. Nobody teaches you these because they are not the phrases you say, they are the phrases you get asked, at speed, while someone waits.

The one that gets everyone is 大丈夫です. It looks like it means okay, so people say it to mean yes, and then they walk out with no bag. It means I am fine, which at a counter is a no.

Four questions, three real answers each. Swipe to see if you had it.
