arigatou is not always the best thank you ⚡

助かりました means that really helped me, and it is what you say when someone actually got you out of a mess, not when they hand you a napkin. すみません means sorry, but it works as thank you too, because it carries the fact that you put someone out to do it. いつもありがとうございます means thank you for always helping me, and it is for the staff at the place you turn up to every week.

Everyone arrives here knowing arigatou and then uses it for all of it, which is fine, except it flattens three different feelings into one word.
