When someone gives you a choice, answer with で.

カウンター席でお願いします. Kauntaa-seki de onegai shimasu. The で tells them you are picking one of the options they just offered you.

When you just name the thing you want, you can use を ください. 生ビールをください. Nama biiru o kudasai. Onegaishimasu also works here, so you are not wrong either way.

And in the て form, ください asks for an action instead of a thing. 温めてください. Atatamete kudasai.
