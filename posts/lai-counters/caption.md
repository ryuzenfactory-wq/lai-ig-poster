In Japan, you count things by their shape. These are the three counting words you will use the most.

Number one. 一枚. ichimai. One flat thing. You use it for tickets, sheets of paper and T-shirts.

Number two. 一本. ippon. One long thing. You use it for bottles, umbrellas and bananas.

Number three. 一つ. Hitotsu. One thing. You use it for most other things, for example when you order food.

If you are not sure which one to use, say 一つ, and people will usually understand you.
