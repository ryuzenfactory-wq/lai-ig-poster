Your textbook says はい means yes, but a lot of the time it only means that the person is listening to you.

On the phone, Japanese people say はい、はい、はい while the other person is still talking. They are not agreeing yet, they are only showing that they are still listening.

So you cannot treat はい as a yes by itself. You have to wait for what comes after it.

When you need a clear yes, listen for いいですよ. ii desu yo. It means the person is giving you permission, so you can go ahead.
