In Japanese, these three times are easy to get wrong.

Number one. 4時. Yoji. Four o'clock. Four is usually yon, but for four o'clock you say yoji.

Number two. 9時. Kuji. Nine o'clock. Nine is usually kyuu, but for nine o'clock you say kuji.

Number three. 7時. Shichiji. Seven o'clock. The standard way to say it is shichiji, and you will also hear nanaji, because shichiji and ichiji can sound alike.
