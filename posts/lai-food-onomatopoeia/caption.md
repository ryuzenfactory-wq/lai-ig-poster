Japanese has a word for every texture ⚡

サクサク is crispy, like tempura or a good pastry. カリカリ is crunchier than that. ふわふわ is fluffy, とろとろ is melty, もちもち is chewy in the mochi way. ぷりぷり is plump, for prawns. シャキシャキ is the crisp of raw vegetables. ホクホク is a hot sweet potato, soft on the inside.

People here do not say delicious as often as you would expect. They tell you the texture instead, and that is the whole review.

Save this one. You will want it at the table ⚡
