Five words for the days when Japan is not going your way ⚡

うそでしょ is what falls out of your mouth when you cannot believe it. ありえない is a step further, for when it should not even be possible. 最悪 is the worst, and it fits a stopped train and a dead phone equally well. 微妙 is how you say something was not good without being rude about it. And かわいそう is poor thing, for the friend whose day went worse than yours.

You will hear all five in one evening in Tokyo. Nobody teaches them because nobody teaches the bad days.
