English has no single word for お疲れ様です. Otsukaresama desu.

You use it to greet the people you work with. Good work is close, but in English it sounds like you are judging someone's work. Thank you for your effort is also close, but it can sound too formal.

You say it when you arrive at work, when you pass a coworker, and at the start of a work call.

When a coworker finishes and goes home, you say the past form. お疲れ様でした. Otsukaresama deshita.
