This is the app I made ⚡

It is called Laifu. It has 1,300 phrases, and they are the phrases people use in Japan every day rather than the sentences from a textbook.

Every phrase has audio so you can hear the pronunciation, and under each one there is a short conversation so you can see how it is used. That part matters. だいじょうぶです means I am okay, but in a shop it means no, thank you, and you only learn that by seeing someone say it.

You can also practise saying the phrases. More than half of the app is free, so you can look before you decide anything.
