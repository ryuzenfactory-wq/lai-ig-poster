You only need three words from a train announcement in Japan.

Number one. まもなく. Mamonaku. Very soon. It means a train is about to arrive or leave.

Number two. 遅れ. Okure. Delay. It means a train is running late, and the announcement usually says how many minutes late it is.

Number three. 運転見合わせ. Unten miawase. Service suspended. It means the trains on that line have stopped for now, so you may need to find another route.

The announcements are long and very polite. When you hear one of these three words, check the departure board.
