These are the three questions staff ask you in the first ten seconds after you walk into a restaurant in Japan.

Number one. 何名様ですか. Nanmei-sama desu ka. How many people? You can just hold up your fingers.

Number two. カウンターでもよろしいですか. Kauntaa de mo yoroshii desu ka. Is a counter seat okay? On a busy night, saying yes usually gets you a seat sooner.

Number three. お待ち合わせですか. Omachiawase desu ka. Are you meeting someone? If you are on your own, say いいえ、一人です. iie, hitori desu.

These are for listening, so you do not have to practise saying them.
