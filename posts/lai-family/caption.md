Family in Japanese 🇯🇵

お父さん otōsan, dad
お母さん okāsan, mom
お兄さん onīsan, big brother
お姉さん onēsan, big sister
弟 otōto, little brother
妹 imōto, little sister
おじいさん ojīsan, grandpa
おばあさん obāsan, grandma

When you talk about your own family to someone else, Japanese people often use plainer words. Your dad is 父 chichi, and your mom is 母 haha.
