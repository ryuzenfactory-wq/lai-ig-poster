Counting to a hundred in Japanese ⚡

いち に さん よん ご, then ろく なな はち きゅう じゅう. After ten it repeats, so にじゅう is twenty and さんじゅう is thirty, and it keeps going the same way up to ひゃく for a hundred.

Numbers come at you fast here. The register tells you the total, the staff asks how many people, the train platform gets called out. If you only half know them you end up nodding at a number you did not catch.
