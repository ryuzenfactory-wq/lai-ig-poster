When you pay in Japan, listen for this one word.

850円になります. Happyaku gojuu en ni narimasu. That will be 850 yen.

円. En. Yen. It always comes right after the number, so when you hear it, you know the number has finished.

After it, you usually hear になります. Ni narimasu. It is just a polite way to finish the sentence, so the price stays the same.

If you miss some of the number, you can usually check it on the register display.
