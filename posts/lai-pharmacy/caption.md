If you get sick in Japan, these are the three questions you will hear at the clinic and the pharmacy.

Number one. どうされましたか. Dou saremashita ka. What seems to be the problem? The doctor usually asks this first.

Number two. いつからですか. itsu kara desu ka. Since when? They want to know when you started feeling sick. You can answer 昨日から. kinou kara. Since yesterday.

Number three. お薬手帳はお持ちですか. Okusuri techou wa omochi desu ka. Do you have a medicine notebook? It is a small book that keeps a record of your medicine. If you do not have one, say 持っていません. motte imasen. I don't have one.
