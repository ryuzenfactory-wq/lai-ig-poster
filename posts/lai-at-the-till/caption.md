These are the three things a cashier in Japan says to you when you pay, and they say them very fast.

Number one. カードでよろしいですか. Kaado de yoroshii desu ka. By card, is that okay? They ask it when they see your card, so はい is all you need to say.

Number two. お支払い回数は？ Oshiharai kaisuu wa? How many payments? They are asking if you want to pay all at once or over several months. Most people answer 一括で. ikkatsu de. In one payment.

Number three. レシートのお返しです. Reshiito no okaeshi desu. Here is your receipt. They say it while they hand it to you, so you do not need to answer. You hear the same words with your change, like 千円のお返しです.

Save this for the next time you pay at a shop in Japan ⚡
