5 phrases for cherry blossom season 🌸 (save this for spring!)

Sakura kirei (the blossoms are beautiful), let's do hanami (花見 / flower viewing), they're in full bloom, did you save us a spot, let's take a photo. Everything you'll say under the trees.

Peak bloom is late March to early April, so tuck this away.
