大丈夫です can mean yes and it can also mean no. That is why it confuses people.

When someone offers you something, it means no thank you. At the konbini they ask if you want a bag, and you answer 大丈夫です.

When someone asks if you are okay, it means I am fine. You trip on the stairs, someone comes over to help, and you answer 大丈夫です.

It is the same word both times. You do not change your tone and you do not add anything. You only have to notice what kind of question you were asked.

If you want to be clearer at the register, say そのままで大丈夫です. Sono mama de daijoubu desu. It means "as it is, is fine", and they will stop asking.
