せっかく. Sekkaku. Most learners have never heard this word.

It means that effort or a special chance should not go to waste. Here are three ways to use it.

1. せっかく来たんだから. Sekkaku kita n dakara. We came all this way. You say it when you want your friend to stay for one more drink.

2. せっかくですが. Sekkaku desu ga. You put this one in front of a refusal. It shows that you appreciate the offer before you say no.

3. せっかくの休みなのに雨. Sekkaku no yasumi na noni ame. It is my day off and it is raining. This time you are only disappointed, and you are not asking for anything.

Save this, and try it the next time you want your friends to stay out longer ⚡
