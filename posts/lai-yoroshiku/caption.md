Yoroshiku means more than nice to meet you. よろしくお願いします. Yoroshiku onegai shimasu.

Your textbook says it means nice to meet you. You also say it at the end of an email, and when you hand work to someone. You even say it in a barber's chair.

It works more like a request than a greeting. You are asking the other person to take care of the rest. So you say it after you ask someone to do something for you.

When you order food, this shorter one is enough. お願いします. Onegai shimasu.
